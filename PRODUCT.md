# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Technical founders and product/engineering leaders preparing recurring stakeholder
updates. This focus was proposed in the review and accepted for implementation.

## Product Purpose
Create and maintain evidence-backed roadmap artifacts showing what shipped,
what is next and what needs a decision. Reduce work on subsequent updates.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One canonical artifact, preserved evidence and grading history, ungraded first
use, reviewed horizon movement, and conditional grade opportunities. Local HTML
and dependency-free Node helpers. No automatic tracker edits or publication.

## Evidence on Hand
Synthetic demo and evaluation fixtures only. No customer testimonials, measured
adoption gains, live project data or participant-study findings are claimed.
