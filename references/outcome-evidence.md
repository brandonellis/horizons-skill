# From work to customer results

Use for initiative synthesis and refresh. Record five separate claims:
customer problem, intended outcome, success measure, delivered capability, and
observed result. Link each observed claim to its supporting evidence and date.

A synthetic example: teams lose time requesting account access; self-service
invitations should reduce that delay; measure median time to first accepted
invitation; the acceptance path is deployed and verified; impact remains
unmeasured until a comparable measurement exists. A ticket marked Done proves
none of the last two claims by itself.

Prefer the team's existing measure and acceptance definition. If absent, record
`Success measure not recorded` and propose a measurement decision separately.
Never invent a baseline, target, revenue impact or attribution. A before/after
change with confounders is an observation, not proof that this feature caused it.
Keep observation period, population and measurement method beside the result.
