# The repeat-use experience

Read for refresh. Inherit scope, audience and authority. Evidence-backed changes
since the previous comparable observation lead; no previous snapshot means no
invented delta. Keep the canonical artifact and preserved history.

Answer these questions in the artifact, with each item opening its source detail:

- What became available to users, with delivery proof?
- What was marked Done but still needs acceptance or runtime verification?
- What reopened, became blocked, or acquired contradictory evidence?
- Which previous claim is stale, and what decision would unlock progress?
- Did measured customer impact change, or is impact still unmeasured?

For starters, `changesSince` and `refreshModel` in `scripts/roadmap-model.mjs`
derive delivery, impact, decision, evidence and approved movement changes. Inspect
changed source content too; a stable source ID does not mean its claim is stable.
For established artifacts, use `roadmap-reconciliation.md`, `feature-rollups.md`
and `refresh-reassessment.md`, including relevant reassessment when graded.

Show useful changes before counts. Keep undated horizons and existing schedule
commitments stable. A refresh never silently moves an initiative or interprets a
closed ticket as a better letter. New planning options remain unplaced pending
a decision. A unresolved grade mapping remains visible and prompts clarification.
